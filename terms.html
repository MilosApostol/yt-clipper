YT Clipper is a personal Mac app that finds highlights in public livestreams, cuts them into short vertical videos and posts them to the YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts its user connects.

Use

The app runs only on its user's own computer. You may use it only with accounts you own or are allowed to manage, and you are responsible for what is posted to them, including following the terms and community guidelines of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok and respecting the rights of the original creators.

Connected accounts

When you connect an account, the app gets only the permissions shown on that platform's login screen. It uses them only to post the clips you make with it and to check which account is connected. You can disconnect at any time in the app or in the platform's settings.
