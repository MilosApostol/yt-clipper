Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

YT Clipper is a personal Mac app by Miloš Apostolović that turns livestream highlights into YouTube Shorts. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, you sign in with Google and allow two things: uploading videos to that channel (youtube.upload) and reading the channel's basic information, such as its name and picture (youtube.readonly). The app does not read your email, contacts or any other Google data.

How it is used

The app uses this access only to upload the clips you make with it, at the times you choose, and to show which channel is connected.

Where data is stored

Your Google sign-in token, settings and clips are stored only on your own computer, in the app's folder. Nothing is stored on any server run by the developer.

Sharing

Your Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. To pick highlights, the app sends transcripts of the public livestreams being clipped to Anthropic's Claude; these contain no Google account data.

YT Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its data by deleting the app's folder on your computer.
