YT Clipper

YT Clipper is a personal Mac app made by Miloš Apostolović. It finds the best moments in livestreams, cuts them into short vertical videos, and uploads them to the YouTube channels of the person using it.

The app runs only on its user's own computer. Signing in with Google lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose and read that channel's basic information (its name and picture).
